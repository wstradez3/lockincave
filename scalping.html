The setup

How it works

  1. Mark the Asia session high and low.
  2. Wait for price to sweep one of them and take the liquidity behind it.
  3. Wait for the 1-minute inversion: the imbalance flips against the sweep, showing the breakout traders are trapped.
  4. Enter on the inversion, stop beyond the sweep, target the opposite side.

Scalping rules

  • Fees matter more here. Every result is counted in R after fees.
  • Maximum two scalps a day. Then the lab is closed.
  • No scalping in the minutes around red-folder news.
  • No scalping on thin days (holidays, weekends), when wicks are random.
  • The scalp never overrides the main daily bias. It's a separate test.
